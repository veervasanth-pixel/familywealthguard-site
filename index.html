Common Questions

The questions every smart family asks first.

If you are considering working with us, these are the questions we hear most often. If your question isn't answered here, your first consultation is the perfect place to ask it.

Is this really tax-free, or is that marketing language?

It is genuinely tax-free — not a loophole, not a gray area, not marketing. When a properly structured Indexed Universal Life (IUL) policy is designed within the limits of IRS Sections 7702 and 72(e), your cash value grows tax-deferred, and every dollar you access through policy loans comes out 100% income-tax-free for the rest of your life. The death benefit to your family is also tax-free under IRC Section 101(a).

These provisions have existed in the tax code for over 40 years. They are the same strategies used by Fortune 500 executives, professional athletes, and high-net-worth families through structures like premium-financed life insurance and private placement variable life. The only difference is that most families have never been shown these tools because traditional advisors are not licensed or compensated to implement them.

We walk you through the exact IRS code sections and carrier illustrations during your consultation. Every claim we make is verifiable in writing.

I already have a 401(k). Why would I need anything else?

A 401(k) is a great starting point, but it is tax-deferred, not tax-free. That distinction will cost most families six figures in retirement. Every dollar you withdraw is taxed as ordinary income at whatever rate exists when you retire — and with the national debt now exceeding $36 trillion, future tax rates are far more likely to rise than fall.

Beyond the tax problem, a 401(k) exposes you to three compounding risks: full market volatility with no downside floor, average management and fund fees of 1-2% annually that quietly erode 30-40% of your lifetime returns, and withdrawal penalties if you need your money before age 59½. You also face Required Minimum Distributions at age 73 that force taxable withdrawals whether you need the income or not.

Our strategies do not replace your 401(k) — they complement it. We help you capture your employer match (never leave free money on the table), then divert additional contributions into tax-free vehicles with principal protection, zero management fees, and full liquidity at any age for any reason.

What kind of results can I realistically expect?

Results depend on your age, health, contribution level, and time horizon — there are no universal numbers. That said, our clients typically retain 30-40% more capital over their lifetime compared to traditional market-based strategies. This is not because our vehicles earn dramatically higher returns; it is because we systematically eliminate the three hidden drags on your wealth: unnecessary taxes, compounding management fees, and market losses during down years.

A 45-year-old professional contributing $1,000 per month to a properly designed IUL can typically project $800,000 to $1.2 million in tax-free retirement income between ages 65 and 85, plus a death benefit passing tax-free to their family. Your actual numbers will differ, which is why we run a full personalized illustration during your strategy session — based on your age, health class, and specific goals.

Every illustration we provide is generated directly from the carrier's actuarial software and is fully guaranteed on the minimum values. Nothing is hypothetical.

Is this safe? What happens in a recession?

Our core strategies are built around principal protection with a contractually guaranteed 0% floor. That means in any year where the market loses value, your account credits 0% — it does not go backward. You never experience a market loss. Ever. This is not a marketing claim; it is written into the carrier contract and backed by the carrier's reserves and reinsurance.

During the 2008 financial crisis (S&P down 37%), the 2020 COVID crash (S&P down 34% in weeks), and the 2022 bear market (S&P down 19%), our clients' protected accounts credited 0% in the worst years and full index participation in the recovery years. A 401(k) investor who lost 37% in 2008 needed a 59% gain just to break even. Our clients needed nothing.

The carriers we work with are all A-rated or better by AM Best and Standard & Poor's, with reserve requirements under state insurance law that are significantly stricter than the capital requirements for banks.

What fees do you charge to work with you?

We do not charge our clients any fees. No planning fees, no management fees, no advisory fees, no asset-under-management fees, no ongoing retainers. Your consultation is free, your strategy design is free, your illustrations are free, and ongoing service is free for the life of your policy.

Our compensation comes directly from the A-rated carriers when we place business with them — this is fully disclosed during your consultation and baked into the policy structure itself. Unlike a 1% asset management fee that compounds against you every year you hold the account, our compensation is a one-time cost to the carrier that does not recur against your balance. After year one, every dollar that grows in your account stays in your account.

Over a 30-year horizon, eliminating a 1.5% annual fee alone can mean keeping an additional $400,000 to $800,000 that would otherwise have gone to your advisor.

I already have a financial advisor. Why switch?

You likely should not switch — and we will tell you that honestly if your current setup is working. We specialize in a very specific side of finance: the tax-free, principal-protected, fee-free side. Traditional advisors generally specialize in the opposite: taxable accounts, market-exposed portfolios, and fee-based asset management. Both approaches serve a purpose.

Most of our clients keep their existing advisor for brokerage accounts, taxable investing, and active trading, while working with us for tax-free retirement strategies, estate planning, living benefits, and insurance. We happily coordinate with your existing CPA, attorney, and investment advisor — and in many cases, your other advisors refer their own clients to us because we handle the specialized work they cannot.

The only time a full switch makes sense is if your current advisor is charging substantial fees for products you could hold directly at Vanguard or Fidelity for a fraction of the cost, and is not providing meaningful tax, estate, or insurance strategy beyond portfolio management. That is a conversation worth having.

Where do these numbers come from?

The daily opportunity cost is calculated by taking your annual income, applying a conservative 18% savings rate, and running that amount through two 30-year projections side by side — one in a conventional 401(k) subject to taxes and market loss, and one in a principal-protected, tax-free strategy under IRS Section 7702. The difference between those two outcomes, divided by your remaining working days, is your daily cost of delay.

The recoverable amount is the projected after-tax wealth gap between those two strategies over your specific time horizon. It is not a guarantee — it is an actuarially grounded estimate based on historical S&P 500 performance, current federal tax brackets, and a 0% floor assumption on indexed strategies.

💡 Why This Number Feels Uncomfortable

Most people have never seen their financial inaction expressed as a daily cost. That discomfort is intentional — not to pressure you, but because the single most dangerous financial decision most families make is the decision to "start next year." Compound growth is the only force in finance that rewards you for being early and punishes you for being late. The clock is not a sales tactic. It is physics.

Would you rather pay tax on the seed — or the harvest? A 401(k) taxes the harvest. A tax-free strategy taxes the seed.
